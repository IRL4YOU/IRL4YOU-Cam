Stand: 10. November 2026

**Kamera und Mikrofon.** Beide Apps nutzen Kamera und Mikrofon des Handys, um Bild und Ton zu übertragen. Es wird nichts auf dem Handy gespeichert und nichts an uns gesendet.

**Hintergrundbetrieb.** Während der Übertragung läuft die App als Dienst mit sichtbarer Benachrichtigung, damit sie bei ausgeschaltetem Display weiter sendet.

**Was du auf der Box mit dem Bild machst**, ist nicht Teil dieser Apps und richtet sich nach deinen eigenen Einstellungen dort.

# Privacy Policy – IRL4YOU Cam and IRL4YOU Cam Net

Last updated: November 10, 2026

**In short:** we do not collect, store or share any data. There are no ads, no analytics or crash reporting services, and no account.

**Camera and microphone.** Both apps use the phone's camera and microphone to transmit video and audio. Nothing is stored on the phone and nothing is sent to us.

**IRL4YOU Cam (simple version).** Transmission goes only through the USB cable to the connected device (the IRL4YOU BOX). The app has no internet permission.

**IRL4YOU Cam Net.** This version may use the network (Wi-Fi, mobile data, USB network) and sends video and audio only to the destination you enter yourself (RTMP, SRT or SRTLA server). The details the server needs to accept the stream (for example stream key, stream ID, passphrase) go there as well. Nothing is sent to us or other third parties. What the server does with video and audio depends on its operator and is not part of this app.

**Settings.** Your choices (for example codec, frame rate, bitrate) and, in the Net version, your saved connections (name, address, key, passphrase) are stored locally on the phone only and do not leave it, except to connect to the server you chose.

**Background operation.** While streaming, the app runs as a service with a visible notification so it keeps sending when the screen is off.

**What you do with the video on the box** is not part of these apps and depends on your own settings there.
